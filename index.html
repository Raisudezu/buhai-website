OUR STORY

BuhAI started in 2025 as a small collection of tools built to help Filipino family members with everyday online tasks — from compressing photos for TNT/Smart data limits to generating QR codes for neighborhood sari-sari stores. Today it's grown into a full platform used by students, OFWs, small business owners, and office workers across the archipelago — still run by a tiny team that believes good tech should be simple, honest, and accessible to everyone.

Full Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Compliant with Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

1. Information we collect

Almost none. Our web server may store standard access logs (IP address, browser type, time of visit) for up to 7 days solely to prevent abuse. We do not link these logs to individual users, and we never sell or share them.

2. How our tools work

Every tool on BuhAI — QR generator, password maker, image compressor, resume builder, etc. — runs entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript. When you generate a password or compress a photo, that data never travels over the internet. It is processed on your device and discarded when you close the page.

3. Cookies & local storage

We use zero tracking cookies. We may store your tool preferences (e.g. preferred units in the BMI calculator) only if you explicitly save them — and you can clear this anytime in your browser settings.

4. Third parties

Our site is hosted on Vercel. Their privacy policy applies to infrastructure hosting only. We embed no ads, no ad networks, and no third-party tracking scripts of any kind.

5. Your rights (RA 10173)

As a Filipino user, you have the right to access, correct, and erase any personal data we may hold. Since we hold almost none, most requests can be answered in one sentence — write to us at privacy@buhai.vercel.app and we'll respond within 5 working days.

This policy is intentionally written in plain English. If anything is unclear, please ask — privacy policies should be read, not ignored.
